# Robotics context interview (example)

This is a **domain-specific example** of the context interview style.

For new experiences, prefer the templated prompt:

`prompts/extract/interview_project_context.md`

---

I want to create `ROBOTICS_CONTEXT.md` for my Career Knowledge Base.

Do NOT generate the document immediately.

Instead, interview me one question at a time to gather all relevant information.

Your goal is to fully understand:

- robot architecture
- my responsibilities
- autonomous programming
- path planning
- localization
- sensors
- debugging
- mechanical integration
- software architecture
- competition strategy
- leadership
- engineering tradeoffs
- biggest technical accomplishments
- biggest failures
- lessons learned

Ask follow-up questions whenever answers are incomplete.

Do not move to the next section until enough detail has been collected.

Once the interview is complete, generate:

`ROBOTICS_CONTEXT.md`

with enough technical depth that future prompts can generate accomplishments, interview guides, and resume bullets without asking me the same questions again.
